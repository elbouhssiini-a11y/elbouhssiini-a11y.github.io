XO Arena Privacy Policy

How the XO Arena app handles information on your device.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy applies only to the iOS app XO Arena, developed by Ahmed Elbouhssini. It does not replace the privacy policy for this website, or the privacy policies of any other app.

The app

XO Arena is a tic-tac-toe game. You can play against another person, against the computer, or through a campaign. The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not ask you to type your name, email, or other contact details into the game. Ahmed Elbouhssini does not run a server that receives your game progress.

Information stored on your device

XO Arena saves the following in the app’s local storage on your device (Apple’s UserDefaults). This information stays on the device. It is not uploaded to a developer server.

  • Coin balance.
  • Shop items you own. These are cosmetic labels stored for the shop. They do not change the rules of a match.
  • Campaign progress, including the highest unlocked level and stars earned on completed levels.
  • Match statistics, such as games played, wins, and draws.
  • Preferences: computer difficulty, preferred mark (X or O), and whether sound effects are on.
  • A count of recent normal matches, used only to decide when a full-screen ad may be shown.

Settings includes Reset Statistics. After you confirm, that action deletes the match statistics stored on the device. It does not delete coins, owned shop items, campaign progress, sound, difficulty, or your preferred mark. Deleting the app from the device removes the app’s local storage with it.

The shop spends coins that were saved on the device. The current app does not use Apple In-App Purchase, and it does not send a purchase to Apple or to a developer server.

Google AdMob

XO Arena uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads from Google AdMob. The formats built into the app are a banner on the home screen, an app-open ad, a full-screen interstitial after some finished normal matches, and a rewarded ad that can retry a failed campaign level. A rewarded ad does not add coins.

The current build requests Google’s published test ads. It is not yet using a production XO Arena AdMob app ID. When an ad is requested, Google may process device and usage information as described in Google’s policies, including information used to deliver, secure, measure, and, where allowed, personalize ads. The app’s configuration also includes Apple SKAdNetwork identifiers so Apple’s ad attribution can work with AdMob.

Google’s policies are here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob & AdSense policies on privacy

Consent and privacy choices

XO Arena uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent information and, when Google requires it, to show a consent form before ads. The app does not force a region and does not add a debug consent device in this build. If Google says privacy options are required, Settings shows Privacy Options. That control opens Google’s privacy-options form. Google’s SDK may store your consent choice on the device.

This build does not show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. More about Google’s consent tools for AdMob on iOS is at Google’s AdMob iOS privacy documentation.

Other services

Besides Apple’s iOS platform and the local storage described above, the third-party service integrated in the app is Google, through AdMob and UMP. The app does not include a separate analytics or account SDK. This policy page is a static file. If you open it on the web, GitHub Pages hosts it and may process ordinary connection data, such as an IP address, to deliver the page. That hosting is separate from the game.

Children

XO Arena does not ask for an account and does not ask you to submit personal details in the game. Ads are requested through Google. Google’s own policies, linked above, describe how Google handles advertising, including settings that can limit ads.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change. The current page describes the app as implemented on October 12, 2026, including test AdMob units.

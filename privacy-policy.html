We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain our applications, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. 8. Pinterest Authorization

You may choose whether to authorize our application to access your Pinterest account. You may revoke authorization through the relevant Pinterest account settings or by contacting us. If authorization is revoked, features that require Pinterest access may no longer function.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.
